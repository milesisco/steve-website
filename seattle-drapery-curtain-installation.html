From hardware to finished panels

The installation plan brings together the treatment's mounting position, its relationship to the window and the way the fabric hangs. For layered curtains and draperies, sharing the complete specification helps Steve understand how each layer should sit in the room.

Rods, tracks and mounting positions

Include the specified rod or track, bracket details and intended mounting height when you contact Steve. Note whether the treatment is wall-mounted or ceiling-mounted, and whether there are existing fittings to work around. Hardware and site details help establish the installation scope before scheduling.

Fabric, fullness and clearance

Share the number of panels, intended finished length and any required clearance around doors, furniture or adjacent treatments. For operable drapery, include how the panels should open and where they should stack. For stationary panels, identify the placement and finished look your design calls for.

Layered window coverings

If the room combines curtains with blinds or shades, send the specifications for both. The space between layers and access to controls are useful details to discuss together. See the separate pages for blind installation and shade installation.

Project questions

Can I contact Steve about curtain installation?

Yes. Whether your specification calls the treatment curtains, draperies or panels, send the details of the design and hardware. Steve works with trade clients on designer-specified treatments; this is an installation service for architects and interior designers.

What should I send for availability or pricing?

Include the project location within the Seattle metropolitan area, number of openings, treatment and hardware specifications, photos of the windows and your preferred installation timing. Steve can discuss the details needed to establish availability and pricing.

Does the work include selecting fabric or making curtains?

This page covers installation of designer-specified treatments. Bring the fabric and treatment design through your designer or workroom, and contact Steve about the installation requirements.
